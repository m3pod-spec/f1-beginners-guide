THE SILVER ARROWS

MERCEDES

Mercedes has been part of Formula 1 for generations, and its modern team became one of the defining forces of the sport's hybrid era.

2026 RACE DRIVERSGeorge Russell · Andrea Kimi Antonelli

THE STORY SO FAR

Where it all began

Mercedes first raced in Formula 1 in 1954 with Juan Manuel Fangio, then stepped away after the 1955 season. The modern works team arrived in 2010 after taking over Brawn GP, which had followed the BAR and Honda entries. Based in Brackley and Brixworth in the UK, the team built an era of its own under Toto Wolff.

THE BIG MOMENTS

What makes this team special

From 2014 to 2021, Mercedes won eight Constructors' Championships in a row. Its Drivers' titles in that period went to Lewis Hamilton and Nico Rosberg. Fangio had also won two titles with Mercedes in the 1950s.

THE CURRENT GRID

The 2026 season

George Russell and Andrea Kimi Antonelli continue as the team's race drivers in 2026, with Fred Vesti named as reserve. The W17 uses a Mercedes power unit. After finishing second in the 2025 Constructors' Championship, Mercedes arrives in 2026 looking to turn that momentum into another title challenge.
