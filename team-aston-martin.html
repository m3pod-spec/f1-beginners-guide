RACING IN GREEN

ASTON MARTIN

Aston Martin's green cars have a much longer family tree than the current team name suggests. The organisation has passed through several identities on its way to the modern grid.

2026 RACE DRIVERSFernando Alonso · Lance Stroll

THE STORY SO FAR

Where it all began

The team's roots run through Jordan, Midland, Spyker, Force India and Racing Point. It became Aston Martin in 2021 after investment from Lawrence Stroll. Its base is in Silverstone, United Kingdom, where the team has invested heavily in facilities and technical talent.

THE BIG MOMENTS

What makes this team special

The modern Aston Martin team scored its first podiums in 2023, with Fernando Alonso taking a series of strong results early in the season. The team has yet to win a Grand Prix under the Aston Martin name, but its ambition and investment have made it one to watch.

THE CURRENT GRID

The 2026 season

Two-time World Champion Fernando Alonso and Lance Stroll remain the team's drivers for 2026. Aston Martin switches to Honda power for the new season. The team finished seventh in 2025 and continues its push to turn its investment into regular front-running results.
