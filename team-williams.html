A RACING LEGACY

WILLIAMS

Williams is one of Formula 1's great independent racing names. Its history is full of championship-winning cars, brilliant drivers and moments that helped shape the sport.

2026 RACE DRIVERSAlexander Albon · Carlos Sainz

THE STORY SO FAR

Where it all began

Founded by Frank Williams in 1978, the team is based in Grove, United Kingdom. Williams became one of the strongest teams of the 1980s and 1990s, winning titles with drivers such as Nigel Mansell, Alain Prost, Damon Hill and Jacques Villeneuve. The team was sold to Dorilton Capital in 2020.

THE BIG MOMENTS

What makes this team special

Williams has won nine Constructors' Championships and seven Drivers' Championships. The 1992 FW14B, driven by Nigel Mansell, is often remembered as one of the team's standout cars. After some difficult seasons, Williams finished fifth in 2025 with 137 points.

THE CURRENT GRID

The 2026 season

Alexander Albon and Carlos Sainz continue together in 2026, racing a Williams chassis with Mercedes power. James Vowles is Team Principal, and Luke Browning is listed as reserve. The team's recent progress has given its supporters plenty to be optimistic about.
