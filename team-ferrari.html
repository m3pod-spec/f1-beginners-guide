THE PRANCING HORSE

FERRARI

Ferrari is the name that has been on the Formula 1 grid since the very beginning. Few teams carry quite so much history, expectation and passion into every race weekend.

2026 RACE DRIVERSCharles Leclerc · Lewis Hamilton

THE STORY SO FAR

Where it all began

Based in Maranello, Italy, Ferrari is the oldest team in Formula 1 and the only constructor to have competed in every World Championship season since 1950. Its story is filled with iconic cars, unforgettable drivers and some of the sport's most celebrated rivalries.

THE BIG MOMENTS

What makes this team special

Ferrari has won 16 Constructors' Championships and 15 Drivers' Championships. Its most dominant modern period came in the early 2000s, when Michael Schumacher won five Drivers' titles in succession from 2000 to 2004. The team's champions also include Alberto Ascari, Niki Lauda and Sebastian Vettel.

THE CURRENT GRID

The 2026 season

Charles Leclerc and seven-time World Champion Lewis Hamilton form Ferrari's 2026 line-up. They race the SF-26, powered by a Ferrari power unit, with Antonio Giovinazzi listed as reserve. Ferrari finished fourth in 2025, so there is plenty of motivation to bring the team back towards the front.
