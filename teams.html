MEET THE GRID

THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE SPEED

F1 is a team sport built around individual drivers. Every team has its own identity, engineering crew and two drivers. Meet the 11 teams on the 2026 grid.

01 · THE DRIVERS

Two seats, one team

Each team fields two race drivers. They compete for the Drivers’ Championship while also scoring points that count towards their team's Constructors’ Championship.

02 · THE CONSTRUCTORS

More than the people in the car

A constructor is the team responsible for designing and building its car. Engineers, mechanics, strategists and many other specialists work together behind the scenes.

03 · TEAMMATES

Working together, racing each other

Teammates share data and help develop the car, but they are also competitors. Their results both count towards the team's championship total.

04 · THE PADDOCK

A whole travelling operation

The paddock is the working area behind the garages. It's where teams, officials, media and other F1 personnel work throughout a race weekend.

✳ PADDOCK NOTE
The Constructors’ Championship rewards the combined results of both drivers — not just the fastest one.
