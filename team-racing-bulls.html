THE NEXT GENERATION

RACING BULLS

Racing Bulls is Red Bull's sister team and a place where young drivers can prove themselves. It has its own identity, its own history and a very important role in the wider Red Bull racing programme.

2026 RACE DRIVERSLiam Lawson · Arvid Lindblad

THE STORY SO FAR

Where it all began

The team is based in Faenza, Italy, and its roots go back to Minardi, which entered Formula 1 in 1985. It became Toro Rosso in 2006, then AlphaTauri in 2019, before taking on the Racing Bulls name in 2024. Several drivers have used the team as a stepping stone to a full-time Red Bull seat.

THE BIG MOMENTS

What makes this team special

One of the team's most memorable moments came at Monza in 2020, when Pierre Gasly took a surprise victory. Its history is closely connected to the development of young talent, including drivers who later became race winners and champions.

THE CURRENT GRID

The 2026 season

Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad make up the 2026 driver line-up. The VCARB 03 uses the Red Bull-Ford power unit. Yuki Tsunoda and Ayumu Iwasa are listed among the team's reserve and test drivers.
