KNOW THE SIGNALS

THE RULES OF THE ROAD

The rules keep racing fair and help everyone understand what's happening on track. Flags are one of the quickest ways officials communicate with drivers.

01 · YELLOW FLAG

Caution: danger ahead

Drivers must slow down, avoid overtaking in the affected area and be prepared to change direction. It warns of a hazard on or near the track.

02 · RED FLAG

Session stopped

The session is suspended. Drivers must slow down and follow instructions to return to the pit lane or another designated area.

03 · BLUE FLAG

A faster car is approaching

A driver being lapped is warned that a faster car is approaching and should allow it to pass according to the rules.

04 · CHEQUERED FLAG

That's the finish

The chequered flag signals the end of a session or race. In a Grand Prix, it is shown to the leading driver when they complete the race distance.

✳ PADDOCK NOTE
flags can have different meanings depending on the context. Officials' instructions always matter.
