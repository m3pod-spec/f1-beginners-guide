GIVES YOU WINGS

RED BULL RACING

Red Bull arrived in Formula 1 in 2005 and wasted very little time making itself known. The team is famous for bold decisions, clever engineering and some seriously successful championship runs.

2026 RACE DRIVERSMax Verstappen · Isack Hadjar

THE STORY SO FAR

Where it all began

The team was formed after Red Bull acquired Jaguar Racing and is based in Milton Keynes, United Kingdom. Its first great era came with Sebastian Vettel, who won four Drivers' Championships in a row from 2010 to 2013. A second dominant period followed with Max Verstappen from 2021 to 2024.

THE BIG MOMENTS

What makes this team special

Red Bull has six Constructors' Championships and eight Drivers' Championships. Its success has been built around strong car design and a willingness to push performance, with Adrian Newey among the influential technical figures in its history.

THE CURRENT GRID

The 2026 season

Max Verstappen is joined by French rookie Isack Hadjar for 2026. The team races an RB chassis with the new Red Bull-Ford power unit. Yuki Tsunoda is listed as reserve after previously racing for the team and its sister outfit.
