A NEW CHAPTER

AUDI

Audi's arrival gives Formula 1 a new full works team from one of motorsport's best-known manufacturers. The name is new to the grid, while the operation underneath it has years of experience.

2026 RACE DRIVERSNico Hülkenberg · Gabriel Bortoleto

THE STORY SO FAR

Where it all began

Audi takes over the Sauber operation for 2026. The team is based in Hinwil, Switzerland, with power-unit development in Neuburg, Germany, and a technical centre in Bicester in the UK. Sauber first entered Formula 1 in 1993 and has raced under several identities, including BMW Sauber and Alfa Romeo.

THE BIG MOMENTS

What makes this team special

The Sauber years include Robert Kubica's 2008 Canadian Grand Prix win with BMW Sauber. The team also helped develop drivers who went on to become major names in Formula 1. Audi now has the opportunity to build on that foundation as a works constructor and power-unit manufacturer.

THE CURRENT GRID

The 2026 season

Nico Hülkenberg and Gabriel Bortoleto form Audi's 2026 line-up. The team finished ninth in 2025 as Kick Sauber and is beginning a new chapter with Audi backing. Its leadership includes Team Principal Jonathan Wheatley and project leadership from Mattia Binotto.
