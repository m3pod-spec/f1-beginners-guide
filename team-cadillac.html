A NEW NAME ON THE GRID

CADILLAC

Cadillac is the newest team on the Formula 1 grid in 2026. It brings a major American automotive name into the championship and starts its story with two drivers who know the sport inside out.

2026 RACE DRIVERSValtteri Bottas · Sergio Pérez

THE STORY SO FAR

Where it all began

The entry is backed by General Motors and TWG Motorsports, with operations spanning the United States and Silverstone in the UK. Cadillac's F1 project was approved in 2025 after a long process to bring an additional team onto the grid.

THE BIG MOMENTS

What makes this team special

Cadillac may be new to Formula 1, but its wider motorsport history includes programmes in endurance racing and IMSA. Its first season is about building the foundations: bringing people together, learning the car and creating a team that can grow over time.

THE CURRENT GRID

The 2026 season

Valtteri Bottas and Sergio Pérez lead Cadillac's first F1 driver line-up. The team begins with Ferrari power while developing its longer-term General Motors ambitions. Its early leadership and technical structure draw on experience from across the paddock.
