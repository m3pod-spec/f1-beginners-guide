PAPAYA POWER

MCLAREN

McLaren has a long history of fast cars and legendary drivers. After rebuilding its form in recent seasons, the team is once again right in the middle of the championship conversation.

2026 RACE DRIVERSLando Norris · Oscar Piastri

THE STORY SO FAR

Where it all began

Founded by Bruce McLaren in 1963, the team made its Formula 1 debut in 1966. Its home is Woking in the UK. Across the decades, McLaren has raced with some of the biggest names in the sport, including Ayrton Senna, Alain Prost, Mika Häkkinen and Lewis Hamilton.

THE BIG MOMENTS

What makes this team special

McLaren has won 10 Constructors' Championships and 12 Drivers' Championships. It also introduced a carbon-fibre composite chassis to Formula 1 in 1981, a major step forward in car construction. The team won the 2024 Constructors' title, its first since 1998, before Lando Norris claimed his first Drivers' Championship in 2025.

THE CURRENT GRID

The 2026 season

Norris and Oscar Piastri stay together for 2026, driving the MCL40 with Mercedes power. Andrea Stella leads the F1 team, while Zak Brown is McLaren Racing's CEO. Leonardo Fornaroli and Pato O'Ward are listed as reserve drivers.
