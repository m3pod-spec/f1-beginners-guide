AMERICAN BUILT

HAAS

Haas is the American team on the grid, with a smaller operation than many of its rivals and a reputation for making the most of its resources.

2026 RACE DRIVERSEsteban Ocon · Oliver Bearman

THE STORY SO FAR

Where it all began

Founded by Gene Haas, the team joined Formula 1 in 2016. It operates from a base in Kannapolis, North Carolina, with its European team based in Banbury in the UK. For 2026, Toyota Gazoo Racing joins as title sponsor and technical partner.

THE BIG MOMENTS

What makes this team special

Haas scored points in its first full season and achieved its best Constructors' Championship finish of fifth in 2018. It is still chasing its first podium and race victory, which makes every strong result a milestone for the team.

THE CURRENT GRID

The 2026 season

Esteban Ocon and Oliver Bearman are the team's 2026 race drivers. Haas continues to use Ferrari power units, while its partnership with Toyota adds another layer to the team's technical programme. Ayao Komatsu is Team Principal.
