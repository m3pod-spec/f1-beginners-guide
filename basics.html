THE STARTING LINE

SO, WHAT IS FORMULA 1?

Formula 1 is the top level of international single-seater motor racing. Here's the quick guide to what you're watching when the lights go out.

01 · THE SPORT

A championship, not just one race

F1 is a season made up of multiple Grands Prix around the world. Drivers race for points at each event, and those points build towards two championships: one for drivers and one for teams (the Constructors’ Championship).

02 · THE CARS

Fast, clever, highly engineered

F1 cars are open-wheel, single-seat racing cars. Aerodynamic wings create downforce, helping them grip the track through corners. Every team designs its own car within the sport's technical rules.

03 · THE GRID

Ten teams, two drivers each

Each team enters two drivers. They compete against the other teams on track, while teammates both contribute points towards their team's championship total.

04 · THE POINTS

Every position matters

In a standard Grand Prix, the top ten finishers score points: 25 for first, then 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 and 1. In Sprint races, the top eight finished score points: 8 for first, then 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1.
