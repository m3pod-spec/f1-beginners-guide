# Project instructions

## Design and writing skills

- Before designing, redesigning, or implementing any user-facing page or interface, read and apply `.agents/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md`.
- Before writing or editing user-facing prose, read and apply `.agents/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md` to remove formulaic AI-writing patterns while preserving meaning, facts, names, numbers, and the intended voice.
- For work that includes both interface design and copy, use both skills.
- Follow the actual project brief and established Circuit Club identity. The skills are guidance, not permission to override the user's explicit preferences or the site's existing design direction.
- Do not force a redesign or rewrite when the task does not call for one.
