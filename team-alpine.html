FRENCH RACING SPIRIT

ALPINE

Alpine's current name is relatively new, but the team behind it has a much longer Formula 1 story. Its roots include some of the sport's most successful eras.

2026 RACE DRIVERSPierre Gasly · Franco Colapinto

THE STORY SO FAR

Where it all began

Based in Enstone in the UK, Alpine is the latest identity of the team that raced as Renault. The Enstone operation also has a Benetton chapter in its history. Renault won back-to-back Constructors' Championships in 2005 and 2006, with Fernando Alonso taking both Drivers' titles.

THE BIG MOMENTS

What makes this team special

The team's wider history includes championship success with Renault and Benetton, as well as memorable individual wins in more recent seasons. Esteban Ocon's 2021 Hungarian Grand Prix victory and Pierre Gasly's podiums are among its modern highlights.

THE CURRENT GRID

The 2026 season

Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto drive for Alpine in 2026. The A526 is powered by Mercedes, following the team's switch in power-unit supply. After finishing tenth in 2025, Alpine has a lot of ground to make up and a clear reason to fight for progress.
